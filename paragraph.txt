Artificial intelligence is changing the way people learn, work, and solve problems. Computers can process large amounts of information and discover patterns that help people make better decisions. Machine learning is a branch of artificial intelligence that allows systems to learn from examples instead of following only fixed instructions. Recurrent neural networks are useful when information has a sequence, such as sentences, speech, and time-series data. They process one part of a sequence at a time and use information from earlier steps to help predict what may come next. For example, a language model can learn patterns from a paragraph and use those patterns to predict the next character or word. The quality of its predictions depends on the training data, the model design, and the amount of training. Even a small model may produce repeated or incorrect text, but it still demonstrates how a neural network learns relationships in sequential data. Practising with simple text is a useful first step toward understanding more advanced language models.
